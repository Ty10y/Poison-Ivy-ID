Key Rules to Remember

  • "Leaves of three, let it be" — poison ivy always has exactly 3 leaflets
  • The middle leaflet has a longer stem (petiolule) than the two side ones
  • Leaflet edges can be smooth, toothed, or lobed — don't rely on shape alone
  • Hairy vines ("hairy rope, don't be a dope") are a giveaway on trees
  • White/cream waxy berries in clusters = danger; never handle or burn
  • All parts of the plant contain urushiol year-round, even bare winter stems
